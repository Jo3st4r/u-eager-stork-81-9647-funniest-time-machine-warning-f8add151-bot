## The label

> WARNING: Do not meet yourself. The last traveller shook his own hand and now neither of them is sure who pays the bill.

1 handshake, 2 of you, 0 bills paid: the label opens like a genuine safety notice — WARNING, do not meet yourself — then turns the real hazard of time travel, a duplicate self, into the joke: the last traveller shook his own hand, and the paradox collapses into the most ordinary problem in the world, a restaurant bill neither of them wants to pay. The surprise is saved for the last few words, and the straight face never slips.
